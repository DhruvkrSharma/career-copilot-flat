## What Recruiters Actually Look For (2025)

### Top 5 Signals (in priority order)
1. **Deployed projects** — Not just notebooks. Live demos, APIs, or containerized apps.
2. **Clean GitHub with READMEs** — Pinned repos, documentation, architecture diagrams.
3. **LLM/GenAI experience** — Even side projects count. RAG pipelines, fine-tuning, agents.
4. **System design awareness** — Can discuss tradeoffs, not just implement tutorials.
5. **Evidence of ownership** — "I built" not "my team worked on." Individual contribution clarity.

### Emerging Requirements
- **Responsible AI knowledge** — Bias auditing, fairness metrics, explainability (SHAP, LIME, Grad-CAM)
- **Multi-modal experience** — Projects combining vision + language (CLIP, GPT-4V, Gemini)
- **Production mindset** — Monitoring, logging, A/B testing, not just training accuracy
