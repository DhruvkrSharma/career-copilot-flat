# LinkedIn Profile Scoring Rubric

**Total Score: 100 points**

---

## 1. Headline (15 points)

| Criteria | Points | Description |
|----------|--------|-------------|
| Role-specific keywords | 5 | Contains target role keywords (AI Engineer, ML, etc.) |
| Value proposition | 5 | Shows what you do, not just your title |
| Not generic | 5 | Not just "Student at [University]" or "Aspiring Engineer" |

### Examples
- ❌ "Student at XYZ University" (0 pts)
- 🟡 "CSE Student | AI/ML Enthusiast" (7 pts)
- ✅ "AI Engineer | Computer Vision • RAG Pipelines • PyTorch | Building real-time ML systems" (15 pts)

---

## 2. About Section (15 points)

| Criteria | Points | Description |
|----------|--------|-------------|
| Storytelling | 4 | Engaging narrative, not a resume dump |
| Technical keywords | 4 | Includes relevant AI/ML keywords naturally |
| Projects/achievements mentioned | 4 | Highlights top 2–3 accomplishments |
| Call to action | 3 | Ends with what you're looking for or how to reach you |

---

## 3. Experience Section (20 points)

| Criteria | Points | Description |
|----------|--------|-------------|
| Impact-driven bullets | 6 | Action Verb + Task + Metric format |
| Relevance to target role | 5 | AI/ML/SWE relevant experience |
| Quantification | 5 | Numbers, percentages, scale |
| Media attachments | 4 | Screenshots, demos, links to projects |

---

## 4. Featured Section (15 points)

| Criteria | Points | Description |
|----------|--------|-------------|
| Projects pinned | 5 | Top 2–3 projects showcased |
| Blog posts / articles | 4 | Technical writing displayed |
| Certifications | 3 | Relevant certs featured |
| Media quality | 3 | Screenshots, thumbnails, not just links |

---

## 5. Skills & Endorsements (10 points)

| Criteria | Points | Description |
|----------|--------|-------------|
| Top 3 skills match target role | 4 | Most endorsed skills are relevant |
| 10+ endorsements on key skills | 3 | Social proof of skills |
| Skills section organized | 3 | Not cluttered with irrelevant skills |

---

## 6. Activity & Content (15 points)

| Criteria | Points | Description |
|----------|--------|-------------|
| Regular posting (1–2/week) | 5 | Active presence on the platform |
| Technical content | 4 | Posts about AI/ML, projects, learnings |
| Engagement (comments, reactions) | 3 | Interacts with community |
| Articles published | 3 | Long-form technical writing |

---

## 7. Network & Social Proof (10 points)

| Criteria | Points | Description |
|----------|--------|-------------|
| 500+ connections | 3 | Threshold for "open networker" badge |
| Recommendations received | 4 | Written recommendations from colleagues/mentors |
| Recommendations given | 3 | Shows reciprocity and professionalism |

---

## Score Interpretation

| Score | Rating | Assessment |
|-------|--------|------------|
| 90–100 | Elite | Recruiters will reach out proactively |
| 80–89 | Strong | Profile stands out, minor tweaks needed |
| 70–79 | Good | Functional but doesn't differentiate |
| 60–69 | Average | Needs significant work to attract recruiters |
| 50–59 | Below Average | Major overhaul required |
| Below 50 | Weak | Essentially invisible to recruiters |

---

## Red Flags

- Default LinkedIn banner image
- No profile picture or unprofessional photo
- Headline is just "Student at [University]"
- Empty About section
- No Featured section
- Experience section reads like a job description (duties, not achievements)
- No activity for 3+ months
- Skills section filled with irrelevant items
- Zero recommendations
