# LinkedIn Profile Examples — Weak vs Strong

## Headline Examples

### ❌ Weak Headlines
- "Student at XYZ University"
- "Aspiring Data Scientist"
- "Looking for opportunities"
- "CSE Student | Tech Enthusiast"
- "Fresher | Open to work"

### ✅ Strong Headlines
- "AI Engineer | Computer Vision • RAG Pipelines • PyTorch | Building deployed ML systems"
- "ML Engineer @ [Company] | Ex-Intern @ [Company] | NLP • MLOps • Production ML"
- "Computer Vision Engineer | YOLOv8 • OpenCV • Edge AI | 3 deployed CV systems"
- "GenAI Engineer | RAG • LLM Fine-Tuning • Agentic AI | Open Source Contributor"
- "AI/ML Engineer | B.Tech CSE @ [University] | Published Researcher | Kaggle Expert"

### Why Strong Headlines Work
- Contain **target role keywords** recruiters search for
- Show **specific skills** (not just "AI/ML Enthusiast")
- Include **proof** (deployed systems, publications, company names)
- Fit within LinkedIn's 220-character limit

## About Section Examples

### ❌ Weak About
> I am a final year CSE student passionate about AI and ML. I am looking for opportunities to grow and learn. I have done some projects in machine learning and deep learning. I am a hard worker and quick learner.

**Problems:**
- Generic ("passionate", "hard worker", "quick learner")
- No specific skills or technologies
- No projects or achievements mentioned
- No call to action
- Reads like every other student profile

### ✅ Strong About
> I build AI systems that work in production, not just in notebooks.
>
> Over the past 2 years, I've shipped 3 deployed ML systems — including a real-time face recognition attendance system (96% accuracy, 500+ users) and a RAG-powered document Q&A pipeline serving 10K+ queries/month.
>
> What I work with:
> 🔹 Computer Vision — YOLO, OpenCV, ViT, real-time inference
> 🔹 GenAI — RAG pipelines, LLM fine-tuning, LangChain, ChromaDB
> 🔹 MLOps — Docker, FastAPI, CI/CD, model monitoring
>
> Currently: Final year B.Tech CSE at [University] (9.2 GPA)
> Previously: ML Intern @ [Company] — reduced model inference latency by 40%
>
> 🏆 Winner, [Hackathon Name] 2024 | Published at [Conference]
>
> I'm actively looking for AI Engineer / ML Engineer roles starting [Month 2025].
> Let's connect: dhruv@email.com | github.com/username

**Why This Works:**
- Opens with a hook (deployed systems, not notebooks)
- Specific metrics and numbers
- Skills organized visually
- Achievements highlighted
- Clear call to action with timeline
- Contact info provided

## Featured Section Examples

### ❌ Weak Featured
- Empty (no items)
- Or: just a link to college website

### ✅ Strong Featured
- 📌 "Real-Time Object Detection System" — GitHub repo with demo video thumbnail
- 📌 "How I Built a RAG Pipeline" — Technical blog post
- 📌 "ML System Design Interview Guide" — Article with 500+ reads
- 📌 AWS ML Specialty Certificate — Credential badge

## Activity Examples

### ❌ Weak Activity
- Last post: 4 months ago
- Content: "Happy to announce I completed a course"
- No comments on others' posts

### ✅ Strong Activity
- Posts 1–2x per week
- "Here's what I learned building a production RAG pipeline [Thread 🧵]"
- "Comparing YOLOv8 vs YOLOv9 for real-time detection [with benchmarks]"
- Comments thoughtfully on AI/ML posts with technical insights
- Shares project launches with demo links
